## 1. 开场问候

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> Please, come here. Good morning, sir.

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> Good morning. My name is Fudil and I was called for an interview with the HR.

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> Oh, I'm Mr. Pan, the human resources director of this company. Oh, great. Please have a seat.

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> Thank you very much, Mr. Pan.

---

## 2. 自我介绍

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> So, first of all, could you tell me about yourself, please?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> Sure. First of all, thank you very much for giving me the chance to have this interview with you. My name is Fudil. My major is marketing. I graduated from the University of Quebec in Montreal. Then the last two years I worked as a trainee in an international company.

---

## 3. 为什么选择本公司

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> What attracts you most to our company?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> I did a lot of research on your company's website and I found that your company was the leading company in the domestic market. So, I pretty admire this as well. Also, the company's values align very well with my personal goals and objectives.

---

## 4. 职业目标

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> Then, what are your career objectives?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> So, first of all, I expect that I can apply my knowledge into practice and also that I can contribute a great deal to this company. Not only as a worker, but also hopefully in the future as a leader of a team of energetic and productive people as well.

---

## 5. 与上司相处的关键因素

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> As a fresh graduate, what do you think is the most important or essential factor to establishing and maintaining a good relationship with your future boss?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> So, all the supervisors have a lot of rich experience. I will try to communicate with them as much as possible if there is any problem or difficulties or challenges within my work, so that we can especially fix those problems or unforeseen circumstances within this position.

---

## 6. 优点与缺点

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> What are your greatest strengths and weaknesses?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> I communicate very effectively with people. I deal with pressure very well and I also maintain a very calm and calculated attitudes. So, I am very excited to be able to apply all of those strengths and qualities into this work, hopefully.
>
> When it comes to weaknesses, sometimes I tend to be a little bit overconfidence and which is not good. So, whenever you have problems, I try to believe in myself to fix it, but sometimes I need help or assistance. And so, it would rather be helpful to ask your supervisors rather than doing it by yourself.

---

## 7. 如何应对压力

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> How do you handle pressure or that type of deadlines?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> I see pressure as a positive thing rather than a negative. First of all, I prioritize tasks. I break them down, I try to remain cool, calm and calculated in order to deal with problems that you may encounter in this position.

---

## 8. 薪资期望

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> Okay. So, what are your salary expectations?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> It all depends on the job responsibilities, tasks and missions as well, but I don't have any specific number in mind.

---

## 9. 反问环节

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> Do you have any questions?

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> Yes, I found out on your company's website that your company organizes business trips overseas. Is that correct?

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> Yes, that's right, but it happens occasionally. You know, we have different markets in different countries. So, it will depend on which departments you will be working in.

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> Okay.

---

## 10. 结束道别

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> So, for now, thank you for your time and we would like to know the result once it is out and I will inform you as early as possible.

### 应聘者（Interviewee）

> Great. I hope we can keep in touch and I look forward to hearing from you soon. Thank you very much, sir, once again. Have a good day.

### 面试官（Interviewer）

> Have a good day as well. Bye-bye.
